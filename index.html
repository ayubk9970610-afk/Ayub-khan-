PRACTICAL KNOWLEDGE

Core OT learning topics

Introductory summaries to guide revision. Actual clinical practice must follow supervised training and local policy.

🧼 Surgical scrubbing

Purpose: reduce transient and resident microorganisms on hands and forearms before sterile gowning and gloving. Follow the product instructions and hospital protocol.

🧤 Sterile gowning & gloving

Maintain the sterile field, avoid touching non-sterile surfaces and use the technique taught by your clinical instructor.

🛏️ Patient positioning

Common examples include supine, prone, lateral and lithotomy. Protect pressure points, nerves, circulation and patient dignity.

🦠 Infection prevention

Use hand hygiene, appropriate PPE, aseptic technique, environmental cleaning and approved instrument reprocessing.

ORAL EXAM REVISION

Viva preparation

Click each question to reveal a short sample answer. Use your course textbook for detailed study.

1. What is an operating theatre?

An operating theatre is a specially designed clinical area where surgical procedures are performed under controlled conditions with trained staff, equipment and infection-prevention measures.

2. What is asepsis?

Asepsis means the absence of disease-causing microorganisms. Aseptic practices help prevent contamination and infection.

3. What is the difference between cleaning and sterilization?

Cleaning physically removes soil and organic material. Sterilization is a validated process intended to destroy or remove all forms of microbial life, including bacterial spores.

4. Name four broad groups of surgical instruments.

Examples include cutting/dissecting instruments, grasping/holding instruments, clamping/occluding instruments and retracting/exposing instruments.

5. Why is patient positioning important?

Correct positioning provides surgical access while helping protect the patient's airway, circulation, skin, joints and peripheral nerves.

6. What is a surgical site infection?

A surgical site infection is an infection occurring in the part of the body where surgery took place. Diagnosis and management require assessment by qualified clinicians.
